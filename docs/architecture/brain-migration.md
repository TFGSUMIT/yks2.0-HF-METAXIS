| Phase | Brain posture | Autonomy posture | Infrastructure posture |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0 | Hosted candidate research | Owned METAXIS scaffold | Provider-owned inference |

cost evidence, failure and recovery proof, and provider-neutral state export.

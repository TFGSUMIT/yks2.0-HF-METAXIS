## Product identity artifacts

The operator-selected METAXIS product lockup and NemaShells skin projection
are versioned under `assets/brand/`.

- Artifact registry: `assets/brand/artifact-manifest.json`
- Primary product identity: `MTX-ART-BRAND-20260714-001`
- Source design language: `MTX-ART-DL-20260715-004`
- Standard Clean reference: `MTX-ART-UI-20260715-005`
- Complex Unpacked reference: `MTX-ART-UI-20260715-006`
- Authority: YKS Ops issue 422
- Proof posture: `manual-placeholder`

METAXIS is the product. SkipJack is the parent brand and appears in the full
skin as `Powered by SkipJack`, not inside the METAXIS product lockup.

Every METAXIS UI must declare `SKIPJACK-CONSOLE-DL-1.0` as its source design
language. The earlier NemaShells-branded mockups are retained as superseded
history and do not govern new UI presentation.
